# Rights Statement

Nepali Archives is dedicated to preserving and providing access to works believed to be in the public domain.

While every effort has been made to verify authorship and copyright status, mistakes may occur. If you believe any material has been incorrectly attributed or included, please contact us and we will review the matter promptly.

No additional copyright is asserted over public-domain works through digitization, transcription, formatting, or presentation.

## Author contributions

The archive also includes works whose authors have explicitly waived their rights
and dedicated those works to the public domain. These appear under author
contributions, separately from literary heritage in the browse pages. Shilaj
Baral's contributed works are recorded on this basis.

Each work records its own rights status and basis. An author contribution does
not make the author's other works public domain. Permission to host a work is
recorded separately from a public-domain dedication; submitting a file alone is
not treated as a rights waiver.
