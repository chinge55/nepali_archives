# Nepali Archives

## Why this exists

Reading Nepali literature on the internet is harder than it should be.

Many important works are scattered across old PDFs, abandoned websites, social media posts, and inaccessible collections. Even when a work is in the public domain, it is often difficult to find, search, read, or download.

Nepali Archives exists to make public-domain Nepali literature freely accessible to everyone.

## Mission

To preserve, digitize, and provide free access to Nepal's public-domain literary heritage.

## Principles

### Free Forever

The archive will always be freely accessible.

No subscriptions.
No paywalls.
No advertisements.

### No Login Required

Reading literature should not require an account.

Anyone should be able to visit, search, read, and download works without registration.

### Public Domain First

Only works that are in the public domain, or works for which explicit permission has been granted, will be included.

Copyright will be respected.

### Preserve, Don't Rewrite

The goal is to preserve original works as faithfully as possible.

OCR corrections should fix recognition errors, not modernize language or alter the author's writing.

### Open Formats

Whenever possible, works should be available in multiple formats:

* HTML
* Plain Text
* PDF
* EPUB

No proprietary lock-in.

### Searchable Knowledge

Literature should not be trapped inside scanned PDFs.

Texts should be searchable, copyable, and accessible for readers, students, researchers, and future generations.

## What We Are Not

Nepali Archives is not:

* A social network
* A publishing company
* A content platform
* An AI content generator

The archive exists to provide access to literature.

## Long-Term Vision

A future where anyone, anywhere in the world, can freely access the public-domain literary works of Nepal through a single, reliable archive.

All should have equal access to the same literary heritage.
